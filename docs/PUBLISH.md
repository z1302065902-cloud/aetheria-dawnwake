**English**

**Aetheria: Dawnwake** is a single-player fantasy real-time strategy game that runs in your browser — no download, no install.

> That dusk, the sky tore open. You fell back to the Green Valley with what is left of the knight order — and the clans deep in the wild are gathering.

**The loop**: gather → build a barracks → train troops → explore under fog of war → fight the Wildborn clans → level your hero → destroy their camp → beat the boss → take the loot → next mission.
15–30 minutes per mission, ten missions across three acts (Embers of the Green Valley / The Forest Pact / Tide of the Void).

**Features**

- **Hero + RTS**: the hero adventures (skills Q/W/E/R, levels, equipment, talents), the army fights, the base grows
- **Not an APM test**: workers allocate themselves (tunable gold/wood/mana mix), production is automatic, army groups carry stances (auto-attack / follow hero / hold) — your attention stays on the hero and the army
- **Ten missions, three acts**: Green Valley (bright grassland) / Dark Forest (dusk mist) / Ruined Fortress (ash night), each with its own lighting, weather and rift events
- **Roguelite relics**: a random run blessing (Blade / Swift / Bulwark / Harvest / March), optional objectives unlock relics, six relics that change how you play
- **Adventure layer**: chests, neutral beasts, a wandering merchant, a scholar, relic vaults, void rifts, meteors
- **Boss fights**: Thornmaw in three phases (roar to summon wolves / stomp shockwaves / enraged charge), the Void Lich, the Ancient Dragon
- **Bilingual**: every string in the game is Chinese + English
- **Zero third-party assets**: all art and audio is generated procedurally in code (16 unit spritesheets with 11–15 frames of animation, region lighting, procedural music and SFX)

**Controls**: drag to box-select · right-click to move/attack · `A` attack-move · `S` stop · `H` hold · `Q W E R` hero skills · `1`–`3` control groups · wheel to zoom · `Esc` to pause.

**Performance**: on an Apple M4 Max at 1600×900 / DPR2, 10/20/40/60 units run at 810/806/818/817 FPS with 0.036→0.083 ms/tick of simulation.

**Known limits (honest)**: three playable heroes (seven planned); no skins yet; portrait phones only guarantee a non-overflowing HUD and the menus target desktop; no multiplayer.
